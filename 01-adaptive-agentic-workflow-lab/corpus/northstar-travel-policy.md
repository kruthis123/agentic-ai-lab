# Northstar Labs Travel and Expense Policy

## Flights

For international flights shorter than six hours, employees may book economy class. For international flights lasting six hours or longer, employees may book premium economy.

Flights should be booked at least 14 calendar days before departure. Bookings made fewer than 14 days before departure require director approval.

## Accommodation

The maximum reimbursable hotel rate in London is SGD 280 per night.
The maximum reimbursable hotel rate in Tokyo is SGD 220 per night.
The maximum reimbursable hotel rate in Sydney is SGD 230 per night.

## Meals

The daily meal limit in Tokyo is SGD 75.
The daily meal limit in London is SGD 85.

## Local transport

Taxi expenses are reimbursable after 10:00 PM or when public transport is unavailable.

## Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 10 calendar days after the trip ends.
A receipt is required for any individual expense greater than SGD 30.
